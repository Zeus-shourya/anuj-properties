How we work

A typical resale purchase, step by step

Every deal is different, but most resale purchases in a Sonipat township follow this order.

  1. Requirement

    You tell us the budget, size, area, facing and timeline. We tell you what that budget realistically gets in each location.

  2. Shortlist and visits

    We shortlist matching properties and visit them with you.

  3. Negotiation and token

    Once you choose, we help agree the price and terms, and a token or bayana is paid against a written receipt.

  4. Document check

    Ownership chain, approvals, dues and any developer transfer requirements are checked before the main payment.

  5. Agreement and payments

    An agreement to sell records the price, schedule and possession date. Payments should go through banking channels.

  6. Registration and handover

    Stamp duty, sale deed registration at the Sub-Registrar's office, mutation, and handover of keys and documents.

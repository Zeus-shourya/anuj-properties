Plot size units in Sonipat: gaj, marla, kanal and acre

Township plots are sold in gaj, farmland in kanal and acres, and old records may mention bigha. Here is how they all convert, using the units in Haryana's revenue records.

Updated 13 October 2026By Anuj Properties, Sonipat

Quick answers: 1 gaj = 1 sq yd = 9 sq ft · 1 marla = 272.25 sq ft = 30.25 gaj · 1 kanal = 20 marla = 605 gaj · 1 acre = 8 kanal = 4,840 gaj = 43,560 sq ft (about 4,047 sq m).

Gaj (square yards)

Residential plots in Sonipat's townships, including Parsvnath City and Omaxe City, are bought and sold in square yards. Locally, a square yard is called a gaj. One square yard is a square 3 feet by 3 feet, so it is exactly 9 square feet.

Marla, kanal and acre

Farmland and village land in Haryana are recorded in acres, kanals and marlas. The Haryana Revenue Department's table of units (for land measured after settlement) works like this:

In consolidated villages, land is also identified by killa numbers. A killa is the standard square-ish field block in the revenue map, generally one acre. You'll see killa numbers alongside khewat and khasra numbers in a Jamabandi.

Not every “marla” is the same. Some people quote a marla as 225 sq ft. Haryana's revenue records use 272.25 sq ft. If a seller describes a plot in marla, confirm the size in square feet or square yards and write that figure into the agreement.

Bigha and biswa in older records

Older (pre-settlement) records may use bigha and biswa. In Haryana's revenue tables: 20 biswansi = 1 biswa, 20 biswa = 1 bigha, and 4 bigha 16 biswa = 1 acre. That makes one bigha about 9,075 sq ft (roughly 1,008 gaj) and one biswa about 454 sq ft. Bigha sizes differ from state to state, so never assume.

Worked examples

  • A 30 ft × 60 ft plot: 30 × 60 = 1,800 sq ft. Divide by 9 = 200 gaj, or about 6.6 marla.
  • A 10 marla house: 10 × 272.25 = 2,722.5 sq ft = 302.5 gaj.
  • Half an acre of farmland: 4 kanal = 21,780 sq ft = 2,420 gaj.
  • A plot priced per gaj: a 150 gaj plot at a quoted rate per gaj costs 150 × that rate, before stamp duty and other charges. See stamp duty and registration charges.

Tips before you sign

  • Write the size in square yards and square feet in the agreement, plus the dimensions of each side.
  • Check the size against the approved layout plan and the sale or conveyance deed.
  • For an irregular plot, the area matters more than the frontage × depth shorthand.
  • For flats, compare carpet area, not super area. See kothis, floors and flats.
  • Measure on site. Boundary walls are not always where the layout says they are.

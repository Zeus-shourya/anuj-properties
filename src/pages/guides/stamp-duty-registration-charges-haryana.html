Stamp duty and registration charges in Haryana (2026)

What you'll pay the government when you register a property purchase in Sonipat: stamp duty rates, the registration fee table, a worked example, and how payment works under paperless registration.

Rates checked 13 October 2026By Anuj Properties, Sonipat

In short: within municipal limits, stamp duty is 7% for a man, 5% for a woman and 6% for a man and woman jointly, on the higher of the deed value and the collector-rate value. The registration fee runs from Rs 100 to a maximum of Rs 50,000, plus a Rs 200 service charge on sale deeds.

Stamp duty rates

Stamp duty on sale deeds within municipal limits
BuyerStamp duty
Man7%
Woman5%
Man and woman jointly6%

These urban rates are made up of the basic stamp duty (5%, with 2% off for women) plus a 2% duty that goes to municipal bodies under the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act.

Outside municipal limits, rates of 5% (men), 3% (women) and 4% (joint) are commonly quoted. Sale deeds in village (Gram Sabha) areas also carry a separate surcharge, so for rural land check the exact figure on the government's stamp duty calculator or with the Sub-Registrar before you budget.

Some government housing schemes have concessions: for example, nil stamp duty on small residential plots allotted under PMAY and the Chief Minister's housing schemes, and a flat Rs 500 stamp duty for PMAY-Urban 2.0 homes of up to 60 sq m. These apply only to the scheme allotments themselves.

What the duty is charged on

Duty is charged on the higher of the price in the deed and the property's value at the collector rate. A property cannot be registered for less than its collector-rate value. If you agree a price below that, you will still pay duty on the collector-rate value.

Buying in a woman's name, or jointly with one, can make a real difference, but ownership has legal and tax consequences of its own, so decide it for the right reasons.

How you pay

Haryana's registration is now paperless. The deed is filed on the e-registration portal, checked online, and the stamp duty and fees are paid online through e-GRAS before you book a slot. Buyer and seller then visit the Sub-Registrar's office once for photographs and digital signatures, and mutation starts automatically. Our documentation support page walks through the steps.

Other costs to budget for

  • Any pending property tax and municipal dues, which must be cleared for the No Dues Certificate
  • The developer's transfer charges on resale in a township, and any maintenance arrears
  • Fees for a deed writer or lawyer
  • Brokerage
  • For purchases of Rs 50 lakh or more, TDS that the buyer must deduct from the payment to the seller and deposit. Ask your CA for the current rate and process.

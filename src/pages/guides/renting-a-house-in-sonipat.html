Renting a house in Sonipat: a guide for tenants and owners

What goes into a rent agreement, how stamp duty on it is worked out in Haryana, when it has to be registered, and how tenant police verification works.

Updated 13 October 2026By Anuj Properties, Sonipat

What a rent agreement should say

  • Names and addresses of the landlord and tenant, and the property's full address
  • Monthly rent, the due date, how it's paid, and the yearly increase
  • Security deposit, and when and how it is returned
  • Who pays electricity, water, maintenance and society charges
  • Lock-in period, if any, and the notice period for each side
  • Permitted use (family residence, office, shop), and whether sub-letting is allowed
  • Who handles which repairs
  • A list of fittings and furniture, with the meter readings at handover

11 months or longer: registration

Under the Registration Act, 1908, a lease from year to year, for more than a year, or reserving a yearly rent must be registered. A lease of less than a year can be registered but doesn't have to be. That's why most homes are let on 11-month agreements that are renewed. Longer leases, common for shops and offices, must be registered.

Stamp duty on rent agreements

In Haryana, stamp duty on a lease is charged at the same rate as a bond (Rs 15 for the first Rs 1,000, then Rs 7.50 for every further Rs 500, about 1.5%), applied to:

  • the total rent for the period, for a lease of less than a year;
  • the average annual rent, for a lease of one to five years.

Tenant police verification

Haryana Police offers tenant verification online through the Har Samay portal, the e-Saral service and the Jan Sahayak app, as well as at police stations. Its published fee is Rs 50, with a time limit of 21 days. Landlords should apply as soon as a tenant is finalised. It protects both sides, and district authorities can make it compulsory by order.

Moving in and moving out

For tenants

  • Pay the deposit and rent by bank transfer, and keep receipts.
  • Photograph every room on move-in day and share the photos with the owner.
  • Note the meter readings and the fittings list in the agreement.
  • Give notice in writing, as the agreement requires, before you leave.

For landlords

  • Check the tenant's ID and work or business details, and complete the police verification.
  • Keep a signed copy of the agreement and the handover checklist.
  • Settle the deposit at move-out against the checklist, with any deductions explained in writing.

Rent and eviction in Haryana's cities are governed by the Haryana Urban (Control of Rent and Eviction) Act, 1973. For a dispute, take legal advice.

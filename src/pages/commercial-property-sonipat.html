What makes a shop or showroom worth more

  • Visibility and frontage on a busy road or the main face of a market.
  • Footfall: how many of the neighbouring shops are open and trading.
  • Parking for customers in front of or near the shop.
  • Floor: ground floor commands the highest rent; upper floors suit offices.
  • Permitted use: the site must be approved for commercial use. Some businesses (food, clinics) need further licences.

Checks before you buy commercial property

  • Title documents and the chain of ownership
  • That the site is approved for commercial use (a licensed commercial site, SCO, or land with change of land use)
  • Approved building plan and occupation certificate for a built shop or building
  • Maintenance and municipal dues paid, and a No Dues Certificate for registration within municipal limits
  • Fire safety approvals where the building's size and use require them
  • If there's a tenant: the current lease, rent, deposit and lock-in

Lease terms to agree in writing

  • Monthly rent, the yearly escalation, and whether GST is extra
  • Security deposit and when it is returned
  • Lock-in period and notice period for both sides
  • Any rent-free period for fit-outs
  • Maintenance and common-area charges, and who pays property tax
  • Permitted business use, signage, and who handles repairs

A lease of more than a year must be registered. See our renting guide for how stamp duty on lease agreements is worked out in Haryana.

Valuation

Property valuation and price guidance in Sonipat

Before you sell or let out, know what your plot, house or shop should realistically fetch. We base the range on what similar properties nearby are asking and closing at.

Ask for a price opinion Call {{phone}}

Price opinion, not a certified valuation. We give you a market view to help set an asking price or rent. If a bank, court or tax authority needs a formal valuation report, that has to come from a registered valuer.

What sets the price of a property here

  • Location within the township: the block, how developed and lived-in it is, and what is next to it.
  • The plot itself: size, shape, facing, corner or park-facing position, and road width.
  • The building: age, quality of construction, number of floors, and whether it matches the approved plan.
  • Paperwork: clear title, dues paid, and transfers in order. Buyers pay more for a property that can be registered without delay.
  • The market right now: how many similar properties are for sale, and how quickly they are selling.

How we work out the range

  1. We note your property's details and, where useful, see it in person.
  2. We compare it with similar properties in the same township and block: what they are asking now, and what recent deals have closed at.
  3. We adjust for what makes yours better or worse: corner, park, facing, condition, approvals.
  4. We give you a realistic asking range, and tell you what buyers or tenants are likely to negotiate.

Collector rate vs market price

The collector rate is the minimum value the district administration sets for property in each area, used for stamp duty. A property can't be registered at less than its collector-rate value. Sonipat's 2026–27 collector rates came into force on 1 April 2026, with increases of up to 75% in some areas, and since 1 August 2026 Haryana's urban property tax is also worked out from collector rates.

The market price is what a buyer will actually pay, and in established townships it is often above the collector rate. Our guide on collector rates in Sonipat explains where to look up the official rate for your area.

After the price opinion

If you decide to go ahead, we'll show your property to buyers already looking in that area, screen enquiries, and help with the paperwork through to registration. If you'd rather let it out, see renting out your property.

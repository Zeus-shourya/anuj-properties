The developer's filings also cover shop-cum-office (SCO) plots and a mall site. Ask us what is currently for sale; it changes often.

Location and getting around

The township lies just west of NH-44, the eight-lane Delhi–Panipat highway, so Delhi is a straight drive south. The KMP (Western Peripheral) Expressway meets NH-44 near Kundli, to the south.

On the transport plans you'll hear about: the Delhi Metro line from Rithala via Narela to Nathupur (Kundli) is under construction and will stop short of Sonipat city, and the Delhi–Panipat–Karnal RRTS (Namo Bharat) corridor is still awaiting final sanction. Treat both as future possibilities, not reasons to pay a premium today.

Buying resale in Parsvnath City: what to check

  • Title: the seller's sale or conveyance deed, or the allotment letter with every transfer since, all in the seller's name.
  • Plot on the layout: plot number, block and size match the approved layout and the ground.
  • Dues: developer, maintenance and property tax dues cleared in writing. Registration within municipal limits needs a No Dues Certificate against the property's ID.
  • Developer transfer: what is needed from the developer's side for a resale (see the note below).
  • Built property: approved building plan and occupation certificate for a house or floor.

Developer status. Parsvnath Developers Ltd was admitted into a corporate insolvency resolution process by the NCLT (Principal Bench, New Delhi) on 30 April 2026, and the admission was upheld on appeal. The company is now run by a resolution professional. If the property you're buying still needs a transfer, no-dues certificate or NOC from the developer, get the current procedure in writing before you pay.

In Sonipat property since 1998

Anuj Properties has been dealing in Sonipat property since 1998, the year written on the board outside our office. We work from the ground floor of Plot No. 449A in A Block, Parsvnath City, Sector 8, inside one of the two townships we know best.

Our focus is simple. We deal in all kinds of property in Parsvnath City and Omaxe City, and we help clients across Sonipat buy, sell and rent plots, independent houses, builder floors, flats, farmhouses and commercial space.

The line on the wall of our reception says “Proficiency in Real Estate”. For us that means knowing the local market well and seeing each deal through its paperwork, not just the handshake.

What we can help with

  • Finding and comparing residential plots, kothis, builder floors and flats, and farmhouses
  • Buying, selling and leasing shops, showrooms and offices
  • Renting a home or shop, or finding a tenant for yours
  • Price guidance before you sell or rent out
  • Documentation: title checks, developer transfer, registration and mutation

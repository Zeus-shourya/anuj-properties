What we help with

  • Document check: title chain, Jamabandi and mutation entries, encumbrance and court-case searches on Haryana's land records portal.
  • Developer side: no-dues letters and the resale transfer or NOC in townships such as Parsvnath City and Omaxe City.
  • Municipal side: the property's Property ID and the No Dues Certificate (NDC) needed for registration within municipal limits.
  • Costs: stamp duty and registration fee worked out in advance, so there are no surprises on the day.
  • Registration: the online deed application, payments, appointment, and the visit to the Sub-Registrar's office.
  • After registration: tracking the mutation and handing over the original documents.

How a sale is registered in Haryana now

Haryana moved property registration online in stages from November 2025 and launched “paperless registration 2.0” in June 2026. In outline:

  1. Get the municipal papers ready. For property within municipal limits, the seller needs a Property ID and an NDC from the ULB property portal.
  2. File the deed online on the e-registration portal, with the property details, buyer, seller and at least two witnesses, and upload the documents. Aadhaar e-KYC is required for both parties.
  3. Deed check. The registration office checks the deed online and approves it.
  4. Pay stamp duty and fees online through Haryana's e-GRAS system, and book a slot.
  5. Visit once at the slot for photographs and digital signatures before the Sub-Registrar.
  6. Mutation starts automatically on registration (since 23 June 2026). Track it on the revenue department's website.

Documents usually needed

Documents usually needed for a resale registration
From the sellerFrom the buyer
  • Title deed and the earlier chain (or allotment and transfer letters)
  • Aadhaar and PAN
  • Property ID and NDC (municipal areas)
  • Developer or maintenance no-dues, and transfer NOC where the township requires one
  • For built property: approved building plan and occupation certificate
  • Aadhaar and PAN
  • Photographs
  • Proof of payments made to the seller
  • Two witnesses with ID

SizeAbout 350 acres, as stated by the developer

HomesPlots, villas (2, 3 and 4 BHK), builder floors, Omaxe Heights apartments
CommercialOmaxe Plaza: shops, offices and a food court, on about 17 acres per the developer

What you can buy or rent here

  • Residential plots in a range of sizes. Listings commonly run from about 150 sq yd to 1,000 sq yd.
  • Villas in 2, 3 and 4 BHK layouts.
  • Builder floors: Omaxe's Swarnaprastha floors (3 BHK, about 1,200 sq ft, ground plus two) and Wisteria floors (3 and 4 BHK on 300–400 sq yd plots), as well as floors built by owners on their own plots.
  • Flats: Omaxe Heights, with 2 BHK apartments of roughly 1,184–1,231 sq ft as listed by the developer.
  • Shops and offices in and around Omaxe Plaza. See commercial property.
  • Homes on rent: flats, floors and villas let out by their owners. See renting in Sonipat.

Location and getting around

Omaxe City and Parsvnath City sit side by side off NH-44, so their road links are the same: a straight run south to Delhi on the eight-lane highway, the KMP Expressway where it meets NH-44 near Kundli, and Sonipat city a short drive to the north-west.

Amenities

Omaxe describes the township as including schools, a hospital, a club, gardens, a shopping complex, security and power backup. Which of these are running, and how well, is best judged on a visit. Ask residents and the residents' welfare association about maintenance and water supply in the pocket you're considering.

Buying resale in Omaxe City: what to check

  • Title: conveyance or sale deed, or the allotment letter with all transfers, in the seller's name.
  • Developer and maintenance dues: a no-dues letter from Omaxe or its maintenance agency, and what the developer needs to record a resale.
  • Municipal dues: property tax paid and a No Dues Certificate against the property ID, needed for registration within municipal limits.
  • For flats: the society's no-dues certificate, parking allotment and the maintenance charges you'll take over.
  • For floors and villas: approved building plan, occupation certificate, and in writing which roof, terrace and parking rights come with the unit.

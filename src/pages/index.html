Plot buying, explained

Why two plots of the same size can carry different prices

  1. Corner plots are open on two sides, so they get more light, air and frontage. They usually cost more than a similar middle plot.
  2. Park-facing plots have an open view and greenery in front, and are usually priced higher for it.
  3. Road width matters: a plot on a wider road is easier to reach and park at. Check the road width on the approved layout plan.
  4. Next to shops or a main road is convenient, but expect more traffic and noise than on an inner lane.
  5. Facing: many buyers prefer east- or north-facing plots for Vastu. Ask for the facing before you visit.

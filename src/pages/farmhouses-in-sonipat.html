Farmhouses and farm land in Sonipat

This page is about buying and selling farmhouses and farm land. We don't book farmhouses for parties or weddings.

What people look for

Sonipat sits right next to Delhi, so many buyers want a weekend farmhouse within easy reach of the city, or land as a long-term holding. Others are farming families buying or selling agricultural land. The questions are different for each, but the checks are much the same.

  • A ready farmhouse: land with a house, boundary wall, gate and usually a lawn or orchard.
  • Farm land for a future farmhouse: agricultural land where you plan to build later. Here, what you will be allowed to build matters most.
  • Agricultural land for farming or holding: where soil, water and road access matter more than anything else.

Agricultural land vs a farmhouse plot

Agricultural land is meant for farming. Building a house or other non-farm structure on it is controlled by Haryana's town planning rules, and generally needs permission, such as a change of land use (CLU) from the Department of Town and Country Planning, depending on where the land lies and how it is zoned in the development plan. A farmhouse that is already built is only as safe as the permissions behind it, so ask to see them.

Checks before you buy farm land

  • Jamabandi and mutations: the seller's name and share appear in the latest record of rights, and the mutation (intkal) entries trace how it came to them.
  • Land identity: khewat, khatauni and khasra (mustatil and killa) numbers match across the seller's papers, the revenue record and the agreement.
  • Demarcation: boundaries marked on the ground by the revenue staff, so you know exactly which land you are buying.
  • Access: a recorded right of way (rasta) to a public road, not just a path across someone else's field.
  • All co-owners: agricultural land is often held jointly. Every co-owner who is selling must sign.
  • Loans and disputes: no mortgage, bank charge or pending court case on the land.
  • Land use: what the zoning allows, and whether any construction on it has the required permission.
  • Water and power: tubewell, canal water, and the electricity connection and its category.

How farm land is measured

Farm land in Haryana is measured in acres, kanals and marlas: 1 acre = 8 kanal, and 1 kanal = 20 marla. In the revenue records land is also identified by rectangle (mustatil) and killa numbers. Our units guide has a full conversion table.

Selling farm land or a farmhouse

Buyers will ask for clean, up-to-date revenue records first. Getting the Jamabandi copy, the mutation record and a demarcation ready before you list saves weeks. Ask us for a price opinion and we'll tell you what buyers are likely to ask.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and share properties that match it.

  • To prepare and check documents for a transaction you have asked us to help with.
  • To keep records we are required to keep by law.

We use your details only for the purpose you gave them to us. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your agreement.

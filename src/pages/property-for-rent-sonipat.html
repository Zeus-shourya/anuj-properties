Looking for a place to rent

Tell us the type of home or shop, the area, your budget and when you need to move. We'll line up places that fit and arrange visits with the owners.

  • Houses and kothis, full or part, for families who want space
  • Builder floors: a whole floor with its own entrance
  • Flats, such as those in Omaxe Heights
  • Shops and offices in township markets and on main roads

Agree these before you pay a deposit

  • Monthly rent, the date it is due, and the yearly increase
  • Security deposit, and the conditions for getting it back
  • Who pays maintenance, electricity, water and any society charges
  • Lock-in and notice period for both sides
  • What the home comes with (fans, lights, geysers, ACs, wardrobes), written into the agreement

Check on the visit

  • Water supply and storage, and the water pressure on upper floors
  • Power backup, and a separate electricity meter
  • Parking, and how far the nearest market and school are
  • Seepage, damp patches and the condition of fittings

Rent agreements in Haryana

Most homes are let on an 11-month agreement. The reason is the Registration Act, 1908: a lease of more than a year must be registered, while one of less than a year doesn't have to be. Stamp duty is payable either way:

  • Lease of less than a year: duty on the total rent for the period, at the bond rate (Rs 15 for the first Rs 1,000, then Rs 7.50 per Rs 500).
  • Lease of one to five years: duty on the average annual rent, at the same bond rate.

Example: 11 months at Rs 15,000 a month is Rs 1,65,000 of rent. Stamp duty = Rs 15 + (Rs 1,64,000 ÷ 500 × Rs 7.50) = Rs 2,475.

Rent and eviction in Haryana's urban areas are governed by the Haryana Urban (Control of Rent and Eviction) Act, 1973. Our guide to renting a house in Sonipat covers agreements, verification and deposits in more detail.

Tenant police verification

Haryana Police offers tenant verification online through the Har Samay portal, the e-Saral service and the Jan Sahayak app, as well as at the local police station. Its published fee is Rs 50. Whatever the local rules, it protects both landlord and tenant, so get it done before the keys change hands.
